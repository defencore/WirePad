WirePad help

Use Wire, then choose a starting pin and a destination. Tap a component to open its searchable pin list, or click a pin directly. WirePad routes the connection automatically. With a mouse, click empty canvas to add a bend. Choose Cancel wire to stop.

To connect two wires, choose Wire, then tap the first wire and the second wire. You can also select a wire and choose Add branch to connect it to another wire or pin. Filled dots mark joined points.

Use Trace and tap a pin, wire, or junction to highlight every connection it reaches. Use Select to move wire segments and junctions. In the Inspector, you can change a wire’s color or use Auto route to redraw it.

On touch screens, drag empty canvas with one finger to pan. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.

Select a component to view its pinout and KiCad assets in the Inspector. Use Copy image to paste the diagram into another app, or use Export to download it.
