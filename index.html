WirePad help

Add and connect

Search the library by part number, board or function, then narrow it by category and manufacturer. Click a part to add it, or drag it into place.

Choose Wire, then a starting pin and destination. Tap a component to open its searchable pin list. With a mouse, click a pin or drag from it. WirePad routes the connection automatically.

To join wires, choose Wire and pick both wires, or select a wire and choose Add branch. Filled dots mark joined points.

Edit your diagram

In Select, pick a wire to reveal its blue handles. Drag a segment to shift it, or move an end to a new pin, junction or wire. Use Add bend, Remove bend or Auto route to adjust its path.

Click to select a component, wire or junction. Drag empty canvas to select a group: left to right selects fully enclosed items; right to left selects anything the box touches. Hold Ctrl/⌘ to toggle items, or Ctrl/⌘ + Shift to subtract.

Drag a selected component to move the group. Arrow keys move the selection; hold Shift for larger steps. Nudge provides arrow buttons. Select one component to change its label or orientation in the Inspector.

New power connections use red for supply and black for ground. Choose a wire color in the Inspector to override the automatic choice.

Explore and share

Hold Shift or Space to use the hand tool temporarily, then drag to pan. Release the key to return to your previous tool and any unfinished wire. A middle- or right-button drag also pans in any tool. Scroll to zoom at the pointer; Shift + scroll pans without changing the zoom. Hold Alt to pan vertically. On touch screens, drag empty canvas to pan and pinch to zoom.

Choose Trace and pick a pin, wire or junction to highlight its connected network.

The Inspector contains each part’s pinout and KiCad files. Use Copy image to paste your diagram into another app, or Export to download an image. Use Project → Save project file to keep an editable copy.
